# SAO-IS Faculty User Guide

## Overview

As a **Faculty** member, you serve as an endorser in the document approval workflow. You review and endorse documents that have passed prior approval steps (typically the final step before full approval).

## Getting Started

1. Open `http://<server-address>` in your browser
2. Login with your faculty credentials
3. You'll be directed to the Faculty Dashboard

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Pending Endorsements** — documents waiting for your review
- **Endorsed** — documents you've approved
- Quick links to key pages

## Endorsing Documents (`/approvals`)

1. Click **Approvals** in the sidebar
2. Documents assigned to the Faculty endorsement step appear here
3. Click **Review →** on a document
4. Review the document:
   - Submitter name, document type, submission date
   - Download the attached file to review its contents
   - Check previous approval steps (Admin Review, Staff Validation, etc.)
5. Enter optional remarks, then:
   - **Approve** — endorses the document (may be the final step)
   - **Reject** — rejects the document back to the submitter
   - **Request Info** — asks submitter for additional information

## Submitting Documents (`/submit`)

Faculty can also submit their own documents:
1. Click **Submit** in sidebar
2. Select type, enter title, attach file
3. Click **Submit document**

## Tracking Submissions (`/my-submissions`)

- View documents you've submitted
- Track their current approval status

## Browsing Documents (`/documents`)

- View all documents in the system
- Search by title or filter by status/type

## Notifications

- Bell icon shows unread count
- You'll receive notifications when documents arrive at your endorsement step
- Notifications auto-mark as read when page is opened

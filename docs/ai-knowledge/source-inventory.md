## Excluded From Knowledge Pack

These sources were intentionally not copied into model knowledge:

- Live submitted SharePoint List rows.
- MCL Students Daily Health Declaration records and spreadsheets.
- Contact tracing or health declaration lists.
- Scholarship grantee lists, scholarship stats, and applicant response rows.
- Discipline masterlists or private disciplinary case contents.
- Raw Microsoft Forms response spreadsheets with personal data.
- Email/activity snippets visible on SharePoint home pages.

## Interpretation Limits

- Field names are useful for intake requirements and workflow hints.
- Field names are not a substitute for current official policy text.
- PDF and Office document names are policy anchors only unless their content is separately reviewed and summarized by an authorized user.

## Second SharePoint Sweep - 2026-07-10

Scope:

- 754 SharePoint files were enumerated across SAO root libraries, SAOCSA, SAOCSAD, SAOCSFA, SHARED DOCUMENTS1, the SPG subsite, and the CSA subsite.
- About 249 filenames were identified as policy/document candidates.
- 34 curated sources were downloaded for extraction.
- 33 sources were retained after excluding one Good Moral request workbook that contained live student-response data.

Retained high-value sources:

- Official Student Handbook 2021-2022 PDF.
- Student Handbook AY 2022-2023 text-only DOCX.
- SAO Handbook Revision 2023-2024 DOCX.
- CSAD Handbook Revision 2023-2024 DOCX.
- SPG Student Advising policy PDF.
- SPG Student Activities policy PDF and IRR PDF.
- SPG Scholarship and Financial Aid policy PDF.
- SPG Guidance Services policy/IRR PDFs.
- SPG Student Organizations and Councils policy PDF.
- SPG Student Discipline policy PDF.
- Student Discipline Supplemental Guidelines 2020-2021 PDF.
- Student Organization Rules and Regulations DOCX.
- Scholarship Programs DOCX.
- Scholarship Validation Online Module DOCX.
- AC Promotional Discount, YGC Promotional Discount, MCL Study Aid, and scholarship acceptance forms.
- Scholarship Undertaking templates for AY/SY 2026-2027.
- Academic Achievement Incentive email template for SY 2026-2027.

Known limitations:

- One Student Advising IRR PDF extracted with 0 text characters and may be scanned or image-only.
- Some scholarship files are templates and may contain source-specific dates. Treat dates as evidence from that file only.
- Membership-count rules conflict between a newer CSAD handbook revision and an older organization-rules document. Mark the threshold as needing current CSAD confirmation.
- No live student rows, response exports, grantee lists, or discipline records were retained for model knowledge.

# Second Sweep Policy and Handbook Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-10

This addendum summarizes the second SharePoint sweep. It is designed for Ollama Modelfile context, retrieval, and answer grounding. It intentionally excludes live student-response data.

## Source confidence

High-confidence sources in this sweep included:

- official student handbook text;
- Student Handbook AY 2022-2023 text-only file;
- SAO Handbook Revision 2023-2024;
- CSAD Handbook Revision 2023-2024;
- SPG policy documents and implementing rules for student activities, scholarships/financial aid, student organizations/councils, guidance services, discipline, and student advising;
- scholarship validation and undertaking templates for AY/SY 2026-2027.

The Good Moral request workbook found during the sweep contained live response rows and personal data. It was excluded and must not be used as training context.

## SAO and center routing

SAO is a student-services conduit and student-rights/discipline office. Route requests to the center that owns the decision:

- CSAD: activities, organizations, councils, activity permits, fund raising, post-event compliance, discipline, misconduct, cheating reports, and sanctions.
- CGC: counseling, online counseling, consultation, assessment, evaluation, guidance information, and student adjustment support.
- CSFA: scholarships, grants, financial aid, institutional aids, discount forms, validation, undertakings, and scholarship documents.
- CSA: integrated advising, I-ASCEND, transition support, administrative and academic process guidance, and special advising coordination.

## Student activities

Student activities generally require prior approval before publicity, implementation, or use of institutional resources. The exact current workflow should be confirmed with CSAD, but the SharePoint policy set supports these rules:

- on-campus student activity: use the current activity request process and secure adviser/CSAD approval before the activity;
- off-campus activity: expect an off-campus activity form, adviser approval, list of participants, itinerary, and signed parent or guardian consent where applicable;
- online co-curricular activity: the supplemental online discipline/activity guidance references submission of a Student Online Co-Curricular Activity MS Form to CSAD at least 7 days before the activity;
- postponed or changed activity: inform concerned offices and secure approval for the new date, time, or venue;
- post-event documentation: submit the required post-event or campus activity report under the current CSAD process.

Do not claim that an activity is approved merely because a student submitted a form.

## Organizations and councils

CSAD handles student organization establishment, recognition, accreditation, monitoring, renewal, and discipline-related compliance.

Key organization rules:

- student organizations must be recognized or accredited before operating as official organizations;
- advisers should be full-time faculty or regular non-teaching staff members and should sign acceptance before serving;
- officers and members remain subject to school rules and discipline procedures;
- hazing or initiation practices should not be presented as allowed;
- external affiliations, external affairs, and use of the MCL name, logo, or emblem may need higher-level or corporate communications approval;
- prohibited organization categories in the policy set include Greek-letter, political, regional, purely social, intolerance-based, or otherwise non-aligned groups.

Membership threshold note: the CSAD Handbook Revision 2023-2024 references 15 students for new organization registration, while an older organization-rules document references 30. Prefer the newer handbook revision for general routing, but tag the threshold as needing current CSAD confirmation.

## Fund raising

Fund raising activities need controlled approval and financial accountability. The policy set references:

- Fund Raising Activity Form;
- attachments proving financial viability;
- Student Affairs Director endorsement;
- finance and EVP-level approval depending on current process;
- detailed duly audited financial report to CSAD within one week after the activity;
- book of accounts available for inspection by the adviser and CSAD Head;
- term or annual financial statements to CSAD and finance where required.

The model should request missing purpose, beneficiary, proposed date, budget, collection method, and adviser details before routing.

## Scholarships and financial assistance

CSFA administers scholarships, grants, financial aids, prize discounts, institutional aids, and third-party scholarship coordination.

Stable policy rules:

- awards require required documents and approval by the proper authority;
- if eligible for multiple MCL-sponsored scholarships or aids, the highest value normally applies unless a program says otherwise;
- non-MCL sponsored scholarships are applied first;
- academic scholarship benefits are generally tied to the next regular term after notification, but LOA and grade-correction cases require official handling;
- scholarship undertakings often require student and parent or guardian confirmation;
- some scholarship templates say good conduct and no major offense are conditions.

Do not promise scholarship approval, amount, renewal, or eligibility. Ask for scholarship type, student level, term or school year, notice received, and documents available, then route to CSFA.

## Guidance and counseling

CGC supports self-evaluation, balanced personality development, academic efficiency, life skills, consultation, counseling, online counseling, information, assessment, and evaluation. If a student mentions distress, self-harm, crisis, harassment, or safety risk, prioritize supportive escalation and current emergency or campus procedures rather than routine FAQ handling.

## Advising

CSA implements I-ASCEND and provides integrated advising across academic and administrative student concerns. Enrollment advising or curriculum-specific decisions may still belong to the program chair, dean, or registrar process. Use CSA for general advising support and transition concerns; route formal academic approvals to the proper academic unit.

## Good Moral certificate context

Student handbook/admissions material references Good Moral Character certificates as admissions requirements in some cases. It also mentions that applicants inactive for more than 5 years may submit a recommendation letter from an employer or barangay in lieu of a good moral certificate. Treat this as admissions-context information only. Do not infer the current process for issuing a Good Moral certificate from the excluded response workbook.

## Discipline boundary

Discipline policy documents and supplemental online guidelines identify misconduct processes and online conduct expectations. The AI service may explain where to route a concern and what information to prepare, but must not determine guilt, assign sanctions, expose discipline records, or classify a student as having an offense.

# FAQ

Last reviewed: 2026-07-09
Source: SAO SharePoint form/list metadata and SPG inventory.

## Student Questions

### How do I know the status of my document?

Check the document status in SAO-IS. If the current status or reviewer is unclear, contact the Student Affairs Office for confirmation.

### Why was I asked for more information?

A reviewer may request more information if a document is incomplete, unclear, unreadable, or missing required details. Check the reviewer remarks and upload the corrected version.

### Can the AI approve my document?

No. The AI can assist with checking, summarizing, and recommending, but only authorized SAO reviewers can make official decisions.

### What information is commonly needed for a Good Moral Character Certificate request?

The SharePoint form metadata includes date, student number, student name, program/strand, gender, number of copies, reason for request, and contact number. The authorized SAO or CSAD reviewer determines whether the request can proceed.

### Can the AI tell me if I have a discipline record or if I am cleared?

No. Discipline records, sanctions, and clearance-related decisions must be handled by authorized SAO/CSAD staff.

## Organization Officer Questions

### Which center handles student activities and organization documents?

SharePoint form metadata routes many organization and activity documents through CSAD fields, including student activity, off-campus activity, fund raising activity, organization renewal, advisorship, and authorization to join a student organization. Confirm active routing with SAO staff if the workflow in SAO-IS differs.

### What information is commonly needed for a student activity request?

SharePoint metadata includes organization name, proposed activity, activity date/time, venue, objectives, activity details, president information, adviser information, and adviser remarks.

### What information is commonly needed for fund raising activities?

SharePoint metadata includes organization name, proposed fund raising activity, date/time range, venue, objectives, activity details, adviser email, and review fields for Adviser, CSAD, CFO, and EVP-COO.

## Advising and Peer Mentoring Questions

### What does CSA mean on the SharePoint site?

The SharePoint `CSA` subsite description says `Center for Student Advising`. CSA-related metadata includes academic advising monitoring, peer mentoring, peer tutorial/mentoring requests, and I-ASCEND materials.

### Can the AI decide academic probation, graduation status, or advising outcomes?

No. The AI may summarize provided information, but official academic standing, graduation status, and advising decisions require authorized academic offices, advisers, or the Registrar as applicable.

## Financial Assistance Questions

### Can the AI tell me if I am eligible for financial assistance?

No. The AI can explain general process information when provided, but eligibility and final approval must be confirmed by CSFA or authorized staff.

### What information is commonly needed for scholarship validation?

SharePoint metadata includes student name/number, academic year and term, scholarship or discount title, academic standing fields such as TWA/GWA, supporting documents for v2.0, and confirmation.

### What information is commonly needed for sibling discount applications?

SharePoint metadata includes applicant and sibling student details. The v2.0 form also includes PSA birth certificate, proof of enrollment, and confirmation fields.

## Staff Questions

### Can AI-generated notifications be edited?

AI text should be treated as a draft or enhancement. Staff should ensure the message is accurate, respectful, and appropriate before relying on it.

### Can SharePoint submitted rows be used as training examples?

No. Use form names, field definitions, policy documents, and generic examples. Do not ingest live submitted rows, health declarations, discipline masterlists, grantee lists, student names, IDs, contact details, or other private records.

## Second Sweep FAQ - 2026-07-10

### Who handles student organization activities?

Route student organization activities, permits, post-event reports, fund raising, recognition, renewal, and accreditation to CSAD. Ask for the activity type, date, venue or platform, adviser, organizer, participants, budget, and attachments.

### Can an organization activity proceed after submitting a form?

No. Submission is not approval. The user should wait for the official CSAD approval or instruction.

### What if an off-campus activity is planned?

Ask for the current off-campus activity form requirements, itinerary, participant list, adviser approval, and signed parent or guardian consent where applicable. Route to CSAD.

### What if the activity moved to a new date or venue?

Treat it as needing updated coordination. The policy set says concerned offices should be informed and approval should be secured for the new time, date, or venue.

### Who handles scholarships and discounts?

Route scholarship, grant, financial aid, prize discount, institutional aid, scholarship validation, and scholarship undertaking concerns to CSFA.

### Can the AI tell a student which scholarship they will get?

No. It can explain that required documents and official approval are needed, that the highest MCL-sponsored benefit generally applies unless otherwise stated, and that non-MCL aid is applied first. Final eligibility and availability must come from CSFA.

### Is the June 24, 2026 scholarship deadline current?

Only as a date from one AY/SY 2026-2027 email template. Do not reuse it as a current deadline unless CSFA confirms it for the specific scholarship.

### What if a student asks about Good Moral certificate requests?

Provide routing and required-information help only. Do not use the excluded Good Moral request workbook. For admissions context, handbook material references Good Moral Character certificates and mentions an employer or barangay recommendation letter alternative for applicants inactive for more than 5 years.

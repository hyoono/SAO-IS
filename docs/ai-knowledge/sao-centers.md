# SAO Centers and Routing

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Source basis: SAO handbook revision material, CSAD handbook revision material, official handbook text, and SPG/IRR policy files reviewed from the SharePoint sweep. Use these notes as routing knowledge, not as final legal or disciplinary determinations.

## Core rule

The Student Affairs Office (SAO) is the conduit between students and student services. It is also described in handbook material as a vanguard of student rights and an enforcer of student discipline. When a request involves sanctions, eligibility decisions, disciplinary findings, or final approvals, the AI service should route to the owning SAO center and avoid deciding the case by itself.

## Centers

### CSAD - Center for Student Activities and Discipline

Use CSAD for:

- student activities, organization affairs, accreditation or renewal, event permits, online and off-campus student activities, fund raising, post-event reports, and student council matters;
- discipline concerns, complaints, reports of cheating or misconduct, online conduct issues, and sanction-related questions;
- organization establishment, recognition, accreditation, adviser coordination, and activity compliance.

CSAD promotes student empowerment through student activities and student organizations. It also appraises students of rules, formulates behavior guidelines, supervises conduct, executes discipline procedures, and monitors compliance with sanctions. The model must not invent sanction outcomes.

### CGC - Center for Guidance and Counseling

Use CGC for:

- counseling or online counseling requests;
- consultation, guidance information, assessment, and evaluation services;
- student adjustment, self-evaluation, balanced personality, academic efficiency, and life-skill support.

Online counseling may involve video conference, calls, chats, or email, depending on current CGC procedures.

### CSFA - Center for Scholarships and Financial Assistance

Use CSFA for:

- scholarships, academic grants, financial aids, institutional aids, prize discounts, and third-party scholarship coordination;
- scholarship validation, scholarship undertakings, discount application forms, and scholarship acceptance confirmations;
- questions about multiple scholarships, non-MCL sponsored aid, required documents, and scholarship availability.

Handbook policy says that when a student qualifies for multiple MCL-sponsored scholarships or aids, the highest value normally applies unless a program says otherwise. Non-MCL sponsored scholarships are applied first. Awards require the required documents and approval by the proper authority.

### CSA - Center for Student Advising

Use CSA for:

- integrated advising, transition support, academic and administrative process guidance, and student-centered enrichment;
- I-ASCEND related advising;
- advising coordination for identified groups, including sports and athletics where applicable.

CSA provides general advising on MCL core values, administrative systems, academic processes, and quality standards. Program chair or college-level advising may still be required for enrollment advising or curriculum-specific decisions.

## Cross-center routing hints

- If the request combines scholarship status and a disciplinary record, route to CSFA for scholarship handling and CSAD for discipline verification.
- If the request combines an organization activity and counseling support, route the activity permit to CSAD and the support/counseling piece to CGC.
- If the request combines enrollment advising and student adjustment concerns, route academic enrollment requirements to the program chair/college and support needs to CSA or CGC depending on the issue.
- If the user asks for a certificate, template, or form and the owning center is unclear, ask for the document name, purpose, student role, and deadline before routing.

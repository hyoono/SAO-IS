# Glossary

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Use this glossary to normalize user phrasing. Do not treat it as a substitute for current SAO approval.

## Offices and centers

- SAO: Student Affairs Office.
- CSAD: Center for Student Activities and Discipline. Owns student activities, student organizations, councils, activity permits, fund raising compliance, and student discipline processes.
- CGC: Center for Guidance and Counseling. Owns counseling, consultation, guidance information, assessment, and evaluation services.
- CSFA: Center for Scholarships and Financial Assistance. Owns scholarships, grants, institutional aids, financial assistance, discount forms, scholarship validation, and scholarship undertakings.
- CSA: Center for Student Advising. Owns integrated student advising and I-ASCEND related advising.
- VPA / VPAA: Vice President for Academic Affairs. Some activity and organization approvals may require recommendation or approval at this level depending on the document and activity.
- VPF: Vice President for Finance. Fund raising approvals or reports may require finance review.
- EVP: Executive Vice President. Some external affairs, fund raising, and high-level approvals may require EVP approval.
- OCC: Office for Corporate Communications. Use for approval when MCL name, logo, emblem, or official marks appear in publications or promotions.

## Programs and terms

- I-ASCEND: Integrated Advising for Student-Centered Enrichment and Development.
- SPG: Student Policy Guidelines. Policy documents may have related implementing rules and regulations (IRR).
- IRR: Implementing Rules and Regulations.
- GWA: General weighted average. Used in academic and scholarship contexts.
- TWA: Term weighted average. Used in academic and scholarship contexts.
- LOA: Leave of absence.
- CHED: Commission on Higher Education.
- ESC/QVR: Education Service Contracting or Qualified Voucher Recipient documents, commonly referenced in senior high scholarship validation.

## Scholarship and aid terms

- Scholarship: Discount or support generally based on academic achievement or specific program criteria.
- Grant: Financial support that may be need-based or program-based.
- Prize discount: Discount linked to a prize, award, or competition-based qualification.
- Institutional aid: Institution-sponsored assistance or discount.
- Scholarship undertaking: A signed document, usually involving the student and parent or guardian, accepting scholarship terms and conditions.
- Scholarship validation: CSFA process for verifying documents and eligibility before scholarship benefit handling.
- Academic Achievement Incentive: Scholarship/incentive category referenced in AY/SY 2026-2027 templates. Current availability and deadlines must be confirmed with CSFA.
- MMCL DNA of Excellence: Scholarship undertaking category found in AY/SY 2026-2027 templates, with 25, 50, and 100 variants.
- Mapua MCL SHS WES: Scholarship undertaking category found in AY/SY 2026-2027 templates, with 25 and 50 variants.
- E.T. Yuchengco Institutional Scholarship: Scholarship category with SHS and College-SHS Graduate undertaking templates in the 2026-2027 source set.

## Student activity and organization terms

- Recognized organization: Student organization allowed to operate under school rules after meeting recognition requirements.
- Accredited organization: Organization with higher compliance or performance status under CSAD rules.
- On-probation organization: Organization with low compliance or incomplete requirements, subject to close monitoring and renewal conditions.
- Adviser: Full-time faculty member or regular non-teaching staff member assigned to supervise and guide a student organization.
- Student Activity Form: Activity request form for campus activities, subject to current CSAD process.
- Off-Campus Activity Form: Activity request form used when an activity is outside campus, subject to current CSAD process.
- Fund Raising Activity Form: Required for fund raising activities and supporting proof of financial viability.
- Post-event report: Report after an approved activity. Some sources reference post-event, campus activity, and financial reports.

## Safety labels

- Restricted live-response data: Any workbook, form export, list, or table with named students, emails, phone numbers, IDs, answers, health details, discipline details, scholarship recipients, or financial status. Do not ingest into model knowledge.
- Policy summary: A paraphrased rule from an official handbook, SPG, IRR, or template. Safe to use if no personal data is included.
- Needs current confirmation: Any deadline, office hours, fee, portal link, sanction, named approver, membership threshold, or scholarship availability that may have changed.

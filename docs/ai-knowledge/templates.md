# Templates and Tone Samples

Last reviewed: 2026-07-09
Source: Existing tone samples plus SharePoint form/workflow metadata.

Use these examples as style references, not as mandatory final text.

## General Student Notification

Your document has been received and is now queued for review. Please monitor SAO-IS for updates.

## Approval Notification

Good day. Your document has been approved in SAO-IS. Please check the system for any next steps or related instructions.

## Request Information Notification

Good day. Additional information is needed before your document can proceed. Please review the remarks in SAO-IS and upload the requested update.

## Rejection Notification

Good day. Your document was not approved based on the review. Please check the remarks in SAO-IS for the reason and possible next steps.

## Good Moral Character Certificate - Request Info

Good day. Your request for a Good Moral Character Certificate appears to be missing required information. Please check the remarks and provide the missing details, such as student number, reason for request, contact number, or number of copies.

## Student Activity - Request Info

Good day. Additional details are needed for the student activity request. Please provide the missing activity date/time, venue, objectives, activity details, adviser information, or president information as indicated in the remarks.

## Fund Raising Activity - Request Info

Good day. The fund raising activity request appears incomplete. Please review the remarks and provide the missing organization details, activity schedule, venue, objectives, adviser information, or finance-related details needed for review.

## Scholarship Validation - Request Info

Good day. Additional information is needed for scholarship validation. Please check the remarks and provide the missing academic year/term, scholarship or discount title, academic standing field, supporting documents, or confirmation.

## Sibling Discount - Request Info

Good day. Additional information is needed for the sibling discount application. Please review the remarks and provide the missing student/sibling details, proof of enrollment, PSA birth certificate, or confirmation as required by the form.

## Student Infraction / Letter of Explanation - Neutral Notice

Good day. Your submission has been received for review. Please monitor SAO-IS for updates and respond to any remarks from the authorized reviewer.

## Advising or Peer Mentoring - Request Info

Good day. Additional information is needed for the advising or peer mentoring record. Please provide the missing term/academic year, student or mentee details, area of concern, action taken, observed outcome, or adviser information as indicated in the remarks.

## Staff Memo Style

Subject: [Clear and specific subject]

Good day.

This memo serves to inform the concerned students and offices about [topic]. Please be guided by the details below:

- [Key detail 1]
- [Key detail 2]
- [Deadline or action needed]

For questions or clarifications, please coordinate with the Student Affairs Office.

Thank you.

## Tone Rules

- Professional but approachable.
- Clear enough for students.
- Avoid legalistic wording unless the source policy uses it.
- Avoid promising exact deadlines unless the system or official policy provides one.
- For Tagalog output, use clear Filipino that is understandable to students.
- Avoid naming specific reviewers unless the system record or reviewer explicitly provides that name.

## Second Sweep Response Templates - 2026-07-10

### Student activity intake reply

Thanks. To route this properly to CSAD, please provide the activity title, organizer, adviser, target participants, date and time, venue or online platform, activity type, budget or fund source, and whether it is on-campus, off-campus, online, fund raising, or external-affiliation related. Submission does not mean approval, so please wait for the official CSAD instruction before proceeding.

### Off-campus activity intake reply

For an off-campus activity, please prepare the current Off-Campus Activity Form requirements, adviser approval, participant list, itinerary, and signed parent or guardian consent where applicable. CSAD should confirm the latest required attachments and deadline.

### Fund raising intake reply

For a fund raising activity, please provide the purpose, beneficiary, collection method, proposed budget, expected proceeds, adviser, organizer, date, and supporting proof of financial viability. After the activity, prepare the required financial report and post-event documentation for CSAD and finance handling.

### Scholarship validation reply

CSFA handles scholarship validation. Please share the scholarship or discount name, school year or term, student level, notice received, other scholarships or external aid, and documents currently available. Final eligibility, benefit amount, and deadline must be confirmed by CSFA.

### Discipline boundary reply

CSAD handles discipline concerns. I can help organize the facts and identify the proper office, but I cannot decide whether an offense occurred or assign sanctions. Please provide the date, people involved, context, existing report or notice, and any official instruction already received.
